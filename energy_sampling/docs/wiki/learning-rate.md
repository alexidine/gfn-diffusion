# Learning rate

*Drift: **M** (mixed). Verified against commit `a637e70`, 2026-09-20. Sources at the end.*

Five config keys name a learning rate, four of them can be reached by one run-level scale, and one controller writes every rate an optimizer group runs at. This page covers those keys, the optimizers they reach, the controller and its tripwire, and what the width and length scaling argument says a rate is. Gradient clipping is a separate bar ([gradient-clipping](gradient-clipping.md)); what a stage transition does to the optimizers belongs to [stage-protocol-engine](stage-protocol-engine.md); the batch a rate is applied over is [batch-size](batch-size.md).

## The rate keys and the optimizers

`train.py::Modeller.init_schedulers_optimizers` builds five Adam optimizers and rebuilds them at every stage transition: `fwd`, `bwd`, `replay`, `fused` and `flow`. The first four take the policy parameters; `flow` takes the Z head alone; `fused` takes the policy parameters *and* the flow head as a trailing param group constructed at `cfg:lr_flow`, so a fused step updates Z through the same optimizer. The four policy optimizers are constructed at `cfg:lr_policy`, `cfg:lr_back`, `cfg:lr_replay` and `cfg:lr_fused` each multiplied by `cfg:lr_control.burn_in_scale`, the value in force for the first train step of a build, before the controller's first `step()` runs.

Which of them is stepped follows from the stage's `train_mode`, not from the key. `train.py::Modeller.step_loss` steps `optimizers[step_type]`, and additionally steps `optimizers['flow']` whenever `step_type` is not `fused`. A `bwd` stage steps `bwd` and `flow`; a `fused` stage steps `fused` alone, its trailing group carrying the flow head. The optimizers a stage does not step are still constructed and still have their rates written and published; they accumulate no Adam moments while the stage runs, and the rebuild at the next transition discards what any of them holds.

`auto` is resolved at load. `utils.py::resolve_derived_config` walks `utils.py::_LR_KEYS` (`lr_policy`, `lr_replay`, `lr_back`, `lr_fused`), replaces any `auto` or absent value with `cfg:lr_control.seed_lr`, and records the names it replaced on `args.lr_servo_managed`. An `auto` rate with no `lr_control` block raises at load (`utils.py::_require_lr_control`, `config_invariants.py::auto_lr_requires_lr_control`). `lr_flow` is not in `_LR_KEYS`, and `auto` is rejected for it at load.

`controller.py::LRController._apply_lrs` writes every group on every controller tick: `lr = base x scale` for a group whose base key is in the managed set, `base` unmodified otherwise, and `cfg:lr_flow` for the flow groups unless `cfg:lr_control.control_flow_lr` is set. `cfg:max_lr` caps every group this method writes, the flow group included; `cfg:min_lr` floors every group except the flow groups taken by the pinned branch, which are written at `cfg:lr_flow` and capped but not floored. `train.py::Modeller.ten_step_reporting` publishes the live rates per optimizer as `lr_fwd`, `lr_bwd`, `lr_replay`, `lr_fused` and `lr_flow`, with `lr_fused_flow` carrying the fused optimizer's trailing flow group; the multiplier and the rail counters are `lr_ctrl/scale`, `lr_ctrl/lr_floored_groups` and `lr_ctrl/lr_capped_groups`, the last only where a cap is configured. The stage action `set_lr_flow:<float>` sets `cfg:lr_flow` at a boundary.

## The controller

The scale is piecewise constant. `cfg:lr_control.mode` is `bracket` or `fixed`; the canonical config sets `fixed`. In `fixed` mode the run holds `burn_in_scale` for `cfg:lr_control.burn_in_steps` steps counted from the stage entry step, derives the hard-failure bars from that window, then moves geometrically to `cfg:lr_control.fixed_scale` over `cfg:lr_control.promotion_ramp_steps` and holds it for the stage; no trials run, and the ramp lives in the checkpointed `lr_ctrl` dict, so a resume continues it. `bracket` mode instead trials `cfg:lr_control.candidate_scales` from the root and promotes a rung `cfg:lr_control.safety_rungs` below the lowest failure. There is no warm-up envelope, no decay leg and no continuous servo under the scale; burn-in is the only cold leg, and `LRController.in_warmup` is an alias of `in_burn_in`.

Divergence is judged by `lr_bracket_probe.py::HardFailureBars`. Per channel in `fwd`, `bwd`, `replay`, `fused`, the loss bar is $\mathrm{hi} + k\,(\mathrm{hi} - \mathrm{lo})$ over the tail `cfg:lr_control.hard_failure.root_window` of the window, with $k$ = `loss_excursion_k`; the gradient bar is `grad_excursion_x` times the largest finite pre-clip norm in the same window. A channel with fewer than `min_observations` finite values gets no bar. `loss_abs` and `grad_abs` are absolute backstops, and a non-finite reading fires by kind.

`LRController.observe` runs on every training step with the step's loss and `last_grad_norm_pre_clip`, appends to the root window and judges. An excursion verdict is gated by a cooldown of `cfg:lr_control.fire_cooldown_steps`, increments `_moderate_fires` and prints `lr_ctrl FIRE`; any other verdict prints `lr_ctrl DISASTER`. Both return `'diverged'` and lead into `train.py::Modeller.fire_loss_spike`, which picks a rewind target in the order same-stage `last_ok`, fresh same-stage `best`, same-stage `running`, this stage's `stage_start`, then bare `best` (`train.py::Modeller._rewind_checkpoint_path`), restores weights, optimizers, modeller state and the metric tracker from it, and keeps `step_ind`. The live buffers are kept. `last_ok` is a link to `running`, remade on the same 50-step clock that writes `running` whenever the combo-loss record's latest sample is at or below the record minimum plus `cfg:rewind_tolerance` times the absolute value of that minimum.

`LRController.on_divergence` is then called once, post-restore. It is the single counting seat for `lr_ctrl/divergences`, and it cuts: the scale, and both endpoints of a ramp in flight, are multiplied by `cfg:lr_control.fire_cut_factor`. The canonical config sets `fire_cut_factor` to 1.0, at which the cut is an identity. `cfg:max_reloads_per_1k_steps` is a rate budget: `fire_loss_spike` raises `FrozenTrainingState` once total rewinds exceed that rate times steps elapsed, floor 3. Where no rewind target exists the cut is taken alone and a warning is printed. The root bars describe `burn_in_scale` while the stage runs at the promoted scale, so `cruise_rederive` queues a refit `cruise_settle_steps` later (`lr_bracket/bars_redrawn`). `LRController.on_stage_change` returns the scale to `burn_in_scale`, clears the windows, the bars and any ramp, and restarts burn-in.

`cfg:stage.hot_lr_sensor` is a per-stage drawdown sensor on one logged channel (`protocol.py::Stage._parse_hot_lr_sensor`, `train.py::Modeller.observe_hot_lr`, `lr_hot_sensor.py::HotSensor`). It pushes the tracker's smoothed value for `channel` into a window of `rows` fresh rows, takes the `floor_percentile` of that window as a floor, and fires on a single row when the drawdown, $\log(\text{value}/\text{floor})$ under `form: log` or $\text{value} - \text{floor}$ under `form: absolute`, exceeds `above`. `action` is a closed vocabulary of `report`, the value taken by omission, which moves nothing, and `fire`, which increments the controller's `_moderate_fires`, takes and re-arms the controller's fire cooldown, drops the sensor's window and calls `fire_loss_spike`. `floor_percentile` defaults to 10.0 and `form` to `log`. `HotSensor` raises unless the `MetricTracker` period the run builds equals the period the thresholds were calibrated at, `lr_hot_sensor.py::CALIBRATED_PERIOD` = 100. The canonical config carries four declarations across its two protocols, all of them leaving `action` at its default: `bwd/mle` absolute over 31 rows above 5.0 on `train_prior` in both protocols, `fwd/scatter_err` over 11 rows above 2.0 on `equilibration` in `unconditional_tb`, and `bwd/logw_std_within` over 11 rows above 3.0 on `var_conditioning` in `conditional_vargrad`.

## Scaling in width and length

`docs/design/width_and_length_scaling.md` derives one distinction. The TB residual $\delta = \log Z + \sum_t (\log P_F - \log P_B) - \log R$ is intensive: $T$ discretizes a fixed horizon, so $\delta$ converges to its Girsanov limit rather than accumulating, and the bounded heads make it width-independent. Anything measured in loss space is therefore $(W, T)$-flat: the Huber knee, the fracs, `deactivate_threshold`, the divergence loss bars. The raw gradient flows through weights shared across all $T$ steps, so it is extensive, growing with $T$ and as $\sqrt{W}$. One value is derived from that: `cfg:gradient_norm_clip` written `auto`, resolved at load against a table of measured pre-clip medians and a $\sqrt{W}$ factor. The note names as the primitives of a rescale the `cfg:model.*_hidden_dim` fields, set equal to each other, and `cfg:integrator.T` with `cfg:eval_T`, which must match it. The note states that the learning rates carry no $(W, T)$ rule, and none is applied in code. Note and code differ on what `auto` means: the note says an `auto` rate is seeded at `adaptive_lr.seed_lr` and owned by whichever adaptive sensor the stage declares; the code seeds it at `cfg:lr_control.seed_lr`, and the stage sensors reach no learning rate.

## Adaptive LR sensing, as of 2026-09-20

A stage may declare `cfg:stage.lr_sensor`, parsed by `protocol.py::Stage._parse_lr_sensor` against `protocol.py::LR_SENSOR_KINDS`, which is `ray` and `none`. `kind: ray` accepts `period`, a positive multiple of 10, and `n_sub`, at least 2; it runs the alpha-star ray calibration (`ray_calibration.py`, batches harvested by `lr_larder.py`), which scores the composite the stage's step descends over a set of step sizes and reports through `raycal/`. `kind: none` takes no other keys. `ray` reaches no learning rate: `LRController.on_calibration` records a count and a last value and returns, and `lr_ctrl/calibrations` is published only when the sensor ran. `kind: hyper`, the hypergradient cosine between the current gradient and the displacement the previous optimizer step realised, was removed on 2026-09-20; it is listed in `protocol.py::RETIRED_LR_SENSOR_KINDS` and a stage declaring it raises at load rather than being ignored. No stage in the canonical config declares an `lr_sensor` block.

## Could be tooling

Two checks are mechanical. The first is a stepped-versus-constructed table: `train_mode` fixes which optimizers `step_loss` reaches and `lr_servo_managed` fixes which keys the scale reaches, so a generator can print per stage which of the five `lr_*` keys is live and which is inert for the stage's whole length. A key inert in every stage reads as a rate and is never applied, and that is derivable at config-generation time rather than at step 0. The second is a resolved-rate print, per optimizer: `base x scale` at burn-in, at the end of the promotion ramp and after $n$ fire cuts, rails marked where they bind.
