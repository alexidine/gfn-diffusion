# Autoregressive spline flow

*Drift: **M** (mixed). Verified against commit `dac8069b` (mxtaltools `b6155c4a`), 2026-11-13; the `mirror_frames` group list 2026-11-15 against commit `1b29b446` plus the working-tree change that adds those maps. Sources at the end.*

A crystal run replaces the SDE policy and $P_B$ with one invertible map on two routes: `inn_modeller.py`, a trainer of its own, and `train.py` with `cfg:model.kind` `nf`, whose policy `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy` (built by `models/nf_policy.py::build_policy`) is a one-step sampler with $\log P_B = 0$. This page is about that map as a density: `models/spline_flow.py::LatentFlow`, or `models/spline_flow.py::ConditionalLatentFlow` on a conditional run, a masked autoregressive flow over the live rows of the policy's state (the crystal latent, or the FD chart's rows when `cfg:energy_config.crystal_coordinates` is `fd`) whose per-row transform is the monotone rational-quadratic spline of `energies/prior_flow.py::_rq_spline`, with a masked-MLP or a causal-transformer conditioner. The `cfg:model.nf` settings carry the names of `inn_modeller.py`'s flags (`models/nf_policy.py::NF_DEFAULTS`), and `models/nf_policy.py::resolve_flow_settings` resolves their unset values.

It covers the three kinds of row and their base distributions, the blocks and their row orders, the exact log-density and how a draw inverts it, the spline inside one bin, the bin-height floor, the two knot-derivative options, which settings live in the weights, the conditional flow, and how the NF route draws and scores a state.

Named and left out: the exact-density trainer of `inn_modeller.py` (its likelihood stage on stored prior rows, its energy stage on the TB residual of fresh draws and stored rows, its replay and its level); the losses, stages and buffers of `train.py` that `NFPolicy` runs under; the latent rows themselves, [latent-dimension-structure](latent-dimension-structure.md); and the crystal prior flow fitted to policy draws, [prior-density-models](prior-density-models.md), which uses the same spline module with free derivatives and the default floors.

## The rows and their bases

`inn_modeller.py::InnModeller.init_gfn` builds a `models/gfn.py::GFN` from the run's config for its geometry only, reads its angular mask, its dead rows and its width, and discards it; `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy` does the same with the run's `gfn_config` and also keeps the GFN's live and dead row indices and the values its dead rows are held at. `LatentFlow` models the live rows; a draw sets every dead row to 0, and no dead row enters a conditioner or $\log q$, so $q$ is a density over the live rows. On the NF route `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy._pin_dead` then writes the GFN's held value into each dead row, of a draw and of a state before it is scored. Each live row is one of three kinds.

| kind | which rows | what the first block reads | base | spline interval | end knot derivatives | per-row shift | outside the interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| wrapped | the GFN's angular rows | the row folded onto $[-1,1]$ by `energies/prior_flow.py::_wrap` | uniform on $[-1,1]$ | $[-1,1]$ | matched, $d_K = d_0$ | rotation, $\mathrm{wrap}(x + \text{shift})$ | none, the input is folded |
| linear | every other live row | $(x-\mu)/\sigma$ | standard normal | $[-b, b]$, $b$ = `--bound` (`cfg:model.nf.bound`) | pinned to 1 | translation, $x + \text{shift}$ | identity |
| bounded | the rows `--bounded_rows` (`cfg:model.nf.bounded_rows`) names | the raw row ($\mu = 0$, $\sigma = 1$) | uniform on $[-1,1]$ | $[-1,1]$ | both learned | none | log-derivative $-\infty$ |

`energies/prior_flow.py::_Flow.set_scaler` (`models/spline_flow.py::_ContextFlow.set_scaler` on a conditional flow, by the same rule) sets $\mu$ and $\sigma$ ($\sigma$ floored at $10^{-4}$) from the rows it is given on the linear rows only and leaves $0$ and $1$ on the wrapped and bounded rows. `InnModeller.init_data_rows` calls it on the training split of the prior rows, and an `--init_from` checkpoint's saved standardisation replaces the fitted one. On the NF route `train.py::Modeller.fit_policy_data_scalers` calls it through `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy.fit_data_scalers` on the latents of every prior row, and skips a policy restored from a checkpoint with `scalers_fitted` set. The standardisation is a fixed change of variable and contributes the constant $-\sum_{\text{linear}}\log\sigma_i$ to $\log q$. `energies/prior_flow.py::_Block` keeps a per-row `bound` buffer, 1.0 on wrapped and bounded rows and `--bound` on linear rows, so a linear row's spline box is $[-b, b]$ in standardised units. With the bounded rows resolved to `linear` (every live unwrapped row), the default of an unconditional `inn_modeller.py` run without `--init_from` and of both NF routes, no live row is linear: every row's base is uniform, every $\mu$ is 0 and every $\sigma$ 1, and every row's spline interval is $[-1,1]$.

On a wrapped row the spline's end knots sit at $\pm 1$ in both input and output, so the spline alone maps the seam point to itself; with $d_K = d_0$ it is a $C^1$ diffeomorphism of the circle. The shift, added before the spline and folded by `_wrap`, is a rotation with unit Jacobian, and being an output of the conditioner it depends on the rows earlier in the block's order. On a linear row the end derivatives pinned to 1 join the spline $C^1$ to identity tails, so the block is a diffeomorphism of the line. A bounded row (`--bounded_rows` takes a comma-separated list of live unwrapped latent rows, `linear` for all of them, `none`, or `auto`, resolved per route as described under run settings below) is a diffeomorphism of the closed interval $[-1,1]$ with no tails: `_rq_spline` returns log-derivative $-\infty$ for an input outside it, so $q = 0$ there, and `energies/prior_flow.py::rq_spline_inverse` never leaves it, a base point of a bounded row lying inside $[-1,1]$. A bounded row takes no shift, and its shift slot is read as its last knot derivative. `models/spline_flow.py::LatentFlow` and `models/spline_flow.py::ConditionalLatentFlow` raise `ValueError` on a wrapped or dead bounded row.

The conditioner does not read the row values directly: `energies/prior_flow.py::_Block._feats` gives it one column per linear or bounded row (the value) and two per wrapped row, $\sin\pi x$ and $\cos\pi x$, which are continuous across the seam. Typed rows (`cfg:model.nf.row_tokens` `typed` or `prefix`, NF route only, on the FD chart) key the transformer conditioner's value and slot embeddings by the row's coordinate type and add to the token of the slot that predicts row $o_j$ a projection, zero at initialisation, of per-row features of row $o_j$ from `models/fd_row_describer.py::FDRowDescriber` (`models/fd_slot_describer.py::SlotRowDescriber` on a mixed-group FD run) computed from the rows before it. `LatentFlow` and `_ContextFlow` raise `NotImplementedError` for them unless the flow has one block, the transformer conditioner and every live row wrapped or bounded, so no typed row is standardised. `tests/models/test_nf_typed_rows.py::test_describer_row_i_reads_only_the_rows_before_it` checks that the features of row $i$ do not change when rows $i$ onward are redrawn, and `::test_typed_conditioner_row_j_does_not_see_rows_from_j_on` the same for a conditional typed block's spline parameters and shift.

Two further row options belong to the same object, both unconditional only (`InnModeller.init_gfn` and `NFPolicy` raise `NotImplementedError` on a conditional run).

`--fold_half_rows` names live wrapped rows whose distribution repeats every half period: `LatentFlow` models $u = 2\,((x+1) \bmod 1) - 1$ on them, and a draw maps $u$ back to $x = (u-1)/2 + i$ with $i \in \{0, 1\}$ chosen uniformly. The fold is two-to-one with $|du/dx| = 2$ and each image carries half the mass, so $q(x) = q_{\text{flow}}(u(x))$ and `LatentFlow.log_prob` carries no correction term. `LatentFlow` raises `ValueError` when the option names any other row, the option is refused with `--w_path` above 0, and `LatentFlow.rsample` raises `NotImplementedError` with it.

`--mix_k` replaces each block by `models/spline_flow.py::_MixBlock`, which on each wrapped row puts, after the shift, the CDF map of a mixture of `--mix_k` wrapped logistics plus a uniform component of weight `models/spline_flow.py::_MIX_EPS` before the spline; its inverse is a bisection (`models/spline_flow.py::_mix_inverse`). `LatentFlow` raises `NotImplementedError` unless the block orders are set (not `none`) and the conditioner is the transformer, `_MixBlock` raises `NotImplementedError` with bounded rows, and the option is refused with `--tied_derivatives` (on both routes) and with `--w_path` above 0. The unconditional defaults of both routes tie the knot derivatives and bound every live unwrapped row, so `--mix_k` builds only with `--tied_derivatives` off and `--bounded_rows none`.

## Blocks, orders and the conditioner

The flow is a stack of `--n_blocks` blocks (`energies/prior_flow.py::_Flow`), each autoregressive over all live rows in one order. With `--block_orders none` the orders cycle with the block index $n$: $n \bmod 3 = 0$ the stored row order, $1$ its reverse, $2$ a permutation drawn from one generator seeded with `cfg:seed` (successive draws for successive such blocks); `models/spline_flow.py::_ContextFlow` builds the same alternation. `--block_orders` sets one order per block, as a comma-separated list of exactly `--n_blocks` entries (`ValueError` otherwise): each entry is `id` (live rows in stored order), `rev`, or latent row indices separated by `.`, listing every live row exactly once. Left unset (`auto`), the flag resolves per route (resolved values: run settings section); on an unconditional one-block flow it becomes `id`, the stored order, cell rows first. `inn_modeller.py::Z1_ORDER`, `8.11.10.9.7.6.5.4.3.2.1.0` (pose first on the $Z'=1$ latent), is taken only when named. On the trainer's NF route the same options are keys of `cfg:model.nf` (`cfg:model.nf.n_blocks`, `cfg:model.nf.block_orders`), `models/nf_policy.py::resolve_flow_settings` resolves `auto` to `id` on an unconditional one-block flow and else to the alternating orders, `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy` refuses explicit orders on a conditional policy with `NotImplementedError`, and `models/nf_policy.py::Z1_ORDER` is the same order, taken only when named.

Within a block, the conditioner reads the features of the block's input and returns $3K + 1$ numbers per row, $K$ = `--n_bins`: $K$ width logits, $K$ height logits, $K$ derivative slots and one shift slot. The numbers for the row in position $j$ of the order depend only on the rows in positions $< j$. The block then shifts each row and passes it through its spline. With $o$ the order, $v$ the block's input (the folded or standardised $x$), $y = T(v)$ its output and $p_0$ the base, a single block ($n_{\text{blocks}} = 1$) is an explicit autoregressive factorisation,

$$q(x) \;=\; \prod_{j=1}^{d} q_j\big(x_{o_j} \mid x_{o_{<j}}\big), \qquad q_j \;=\; p_0\big(y_{o_j}\big)\,\Big|\frac{\partial y_{o_j}}{\partial v_{o_j}}\Big|\,\frac{1}{\sigma_{o_j}},$$

each factor a normalised one-dimensional density for every value of the preceding rows, since given them $y_{o_j}$ is a bijection of the row's domain (the circle on a wrapped row, the line on a linear row, $[-1,1]$ on a bounded row) onto the base's support with positive derivative, and the base factorises over rows. A stack of blocks in different orders is a composition of such maps; its density is still exact, and is not in general an autoregressive factorisation in one order.

Two conditioners exist. `--conditioner made` is `energies/prior_flow.py::_MADE`: masked linear layers of widths `--hidden` repeated `--hidden_layers` times, with SiLU, the sin and cos columns of a wrapped row sharing one autoregressive degree, the output for a row masked to depend only on rows strictly earlier in the order. `--conditioner transformer` (the default) replaces each block's MADE by `energies/ar_transformer.py::CausalTransformerConditioner`: one token per slot of the order, slot $j$ carrying a linear embedding of the features of row $o_{j-1}$ plus a learned embedding of which row that is, slot 0 carrying a learned start token, every slot adding a learned per-slot embedding, which under the block's fixed order names the row the slot predicts; `--tf_layers` pre-norm layers of causal self-attention and a four-times-wide MLP (the NF route's `tf_mlp_ratio`, default 4, `models/nf_policy.py::NF_PENDING_DEFAULTS`) at width `--tf_width` with `--tf_heads` heads; a final normalisation and a head shared by all slots giving $3K+1$ numbers, reordered from slot order to row order. Causal masking gives slot $j$ the tokens of slots $\le j$, which carry rows $o_{<j}$ only. The normalisation before each layer's self-attention, cross-attention and MLP, and the final one before the head, is LayerNorm (`--tf_norm layer`), RMSNorm (`rms`) or the identity (`none`), built by `energies/ar_transformer.py::_norm` from `::NORMS`. On CUDA the layers run in bfloat16 and the head and spline in the input's precision; `--tf_fp32` runs the layers in the input's precision and is refused with the MADE conditioner (`InnModeller.init_gfn`, `NotImplementedError`); on the NF route `cfg:model.nf.tf_fp32` with the MADE conditioner is not refused and changes nothing.

Both heads start at zero weights, zero bias on the width, height and shift slots, and bias $\kappa$ = `energies/prior_flow.py::_SOFTPLUS_ONE` $= \log(e-1)$ on the derivative slots, for which $\mathrm{softplus}(\kappa) = 1$; every bin is then uniform and every shift zero. Under free derivatives a learned knot derivative is then $1 + d_{\min}$, with $d_{\min}$ = `_MIN_DERIV` $= 10^{-3}$, so each block is the identity to within a log-derivative of order $d_{\min}$; under tied derivatives the factor is exactly 1 and the bin slopes are all 1, so each block is exactly the identity.

## Density: the forward direction

The forward direction maps data to base. `energies/prior_flow.py::_Flow.log_prob`, which `LatentFlow.log_prob` calls on the live rows, folds the wrapped rows, standardises the linear rows, runs every block once and returns

$$\log q(x) \;=\; \sum_{i}\log p_{0,i}(\zeta_i) \;+\; \sum_{\text{blocks}}\sum_i \log\Big|\frac{\partial y_i}{\partial v_i}\Big| \;-\; \sum_{\text{linear } i}\log\sigma_i,$$

with $v$ and $y$ each block's input and output, $\zeta$ the last block's output (the base point), and $\log p_{0,i}(\zeta_i) = -\log 2$ on wrapped and bounded rows and $-\tfrac12 \zeta_i^2 - \tfrac12\log 2\pi$ on linear rows. The normaliser is exact by construction and evaluating it costs one conditioner pass per block. `models/spline_flow.py::ConditionalLatentFlow.log_prob` returns the same sum through `ConditionalLatentFlow.to_base`, every block reading the condition's context.

`inn_modeller.py::InnModeller.per_dim_log_q` returns the same sum split by live row: each row's spline log-derivatives summed over blocks, its standardisation term and its base term. On unconditional runs it sums to `log_prob`, term by term from the formula above; `InnModeller.dump_draws` raises `RuntimeError` when the two differ by more than $10^{-2}$ on a draw. At $n_{\text{blocks}} = 1$ each row's term is $\log q_j$ above, the conditional log-density of that row given the rows before it in the order; in a stack a row's term sums its log-derivatives over blocks, each conditional on a different set of rows.

## Sampling: the inverse direction

`LatentFlow.sample` draws a base point (uniform on $[-1,1]$ on wrapped and bounded rows, standard normal on linear rows), inverts the blocks from last to first (a block with the causal-transformer conditioner through `models/spline_flow.py::invert_block_cached`, with typed rows also handed `LatentFlow.row_extra_step`; a MADE block through `energies/prior_flow.py::invert_block`; a mixture block through `models/spline_flow.py::_MixBlock.invert`), folds the wrapped rows, unstandardises the linear rows, writes the live rows into a zero vector and unfolds any half-period rows. `ConditionalLatentFlow.sample` does the same with the block's context, a MADE block through `models/spline_flow.py::_ContextBlock.invert`.

`invert_block` starts from $x = 0$ and applies $d$ times, $d$ the number of live rows, the update $x \leftarrow T^{-1}\big(y;\ \theta(x)\big)$: run the conditioner on the current $x$, invert every row's spline at $y$ with those parameters (`rq_spline_inverse`, a quadratic within one bin, given at the end of the section on the spline in one bin), and undo the shift. The parameters of row $o_1$ depend on no row, so the first pass makes $x_{o_1}$ exact. If after pass $p$ the rows $o_1, \dots, o_p$ are exact, then in pass $p+1$ the parameters of row $o_{p+1}$ are computed from exact values and $x_{o_{p+1}}$ becomes exact, while rows $o_{\le p}$ stay exact, their parameters reading only exact rows. After $d$ passes every row is exact, whatever the order, and the order is never read. On a MADE block a draw therefore costs $d$ conditioner passes against one for $\log q$. `_ContextBlock.invert` is the same $d$-pass iteration with the context read on every pass.

`invert_block_cached` makes the same per-row updates in the order's sequence, one conditioner step per row. `energies/ar_transformer.py::CausalTransformerConditioner.decode` keeps every layer's keys and values for the slots already computed; step $j$ computes slot $j$'s token alone (the start token at $j = 0$, else the value embedding of row $o_{j-1}$, fixed at the step before, plus the slot embedding, on a typed-row conditioner the per-row features of row $o_j$ from the rows already fixed, and on a conditional one the context's projection), which attends to the cached slots and to itself, so it is the computation the full pass makes for that slot on the completed rows. The head gives row $o_j$'s parameters, the row's spline is inverted at its value in $y$, the block's output, the shift is undone, and the row's features enter the next token. The work is about one conditioner pass per block, in $d$ sequential steps, and the rows equal the $d$-pass inverse's up to rounding.

On CUDA without `--tf_fp32` both paths run the layers under bfloat16 autocast, the decode one token at a time and the full pass over all slots at once, so they round differently. `tests/models/test_inn_cached_sampler.py::test_cached_equals_dpass_unbounded_and_bounded` compares the two inverses in double precision on a one-block 12-row flow in the order `Z1_ORDER` whose transformer conditioner's head and embeddings are moved away from the identity, with tied derivatives and floor $10^{-7}$, with and without bounded rows: the 99.9th percentile of the difference below $10^{-10}$, its maximum below $10^{-5}$, and the block's forward pass on the cached inverse within $10^{-9}$ of the base points; `::test_negative_control_conditioner_input_matters` perturbs one slot embedding after the cached draw and requires the $d$-pass inverse to move by more than $10^{-3}$.

`decode` raises `ValueError` when a context is passed to an unconditional conditioner or missing from a conditional one, when a cross-attending conditioner is not given its memory of tokens or another is given one, and when per-row features are given to a conditioner built without them or withheld from one built with them (see Conditional runs).

`models/spline_flow.py::invert_differentiable` runs the same $d$ passes with the autograd graph kept, and `LatentFlow.rsample` (and `ConditionalLatentFlow.rsample`, with the context) composes it over the blocks to give the draw `sample` makes from given base points, differentiable in the parameters; on a transformer block it runs $d$ full passes where `sample` decodes through the cache, so the two draws agree up to rounding. `LatentFlow.rsample` raises `NotImplementedError` with `--fold_half_rows` and with `--mix_k`. After pass $p \ge j$ row $o_j$ is, as a function of the parameters, the exact inverse's row, since it reads only rows $o_{<j}$, exact from pass $j-1$ on; the $d$-pass map therefore coincides with the exact inverse for every parameter value, and the derivative autograd takes through the passes is the derivative of the exact inverse. The trainer's NF route takes no pathwise gradient through a draw: `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy.get_traj_fwd` raises `NotImplementedError` when asked for one.

`inn_modeller.py::InnModeller.check_sampler_round_trip` runs at start-up, after any `--init_from` load: on a double-precision copy of the flow and then on the flow itself, it draws 4096 base points in chunks of 512 (on a conditional run with training conditions drawn at random), samples, maps the draws back with the sampler's `to_base` (`LatentFlow.to_base` or `ConditionalLatentFlow.to_base`), takes per draw the largest error over live rows (folded on wrapped rows), prints `sampler round trip, ...`, and raises `RuntimeError` unless the largest error on the double-precision copy is below $10^{-6}$ and the 99th percentile of the working-precision error is below $10^{-2}$ base units. The trainer's NF route runs no such check: `NFPolicy.get_traj_fwd` draws with `sample` and `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy._one_step` scores each draw with `log_prob` on the drawn rows.

## The rational-quadratic spline in one bin

`energies/prior_flow.py::_bin_fractions` turns the width and height logits of a row into fractions $\ell + (1 - K\ell)\,\mathrm{softmax}(\cdot)$, each at least $\ell$ and summing to 1, with $\ell = \ell_0$ = `_MIN_BIN` $= 10^{-3}$ for the widths and $\ell_0$ or the row's height floor for the heights. `_rq_spline` takes their cumulative sums, normalises the last to exactly 1 and maps them onto $[-b, b]$, giving knots $(x_k, y_k)$, $k = 0, \dots, K$, bin widths $w_k$, heights $h_k$ and mean slopes $s_k = h_k / w_k$. Both coordinates are scaled by the same $2b$, so $s_k$ is the ratio of the bin's height fraction to its width fraction. With knot derivatives $d_k$ and $\xi = (x - x_k)/w_k \in [0,1]$ for $x$ in bin $k$, writing $s$ for $s_k$ and $h$ for $h_k$,

$$y \;=\; y_k + h\,\frac{s\xi^2 + d_k\,\xi(1-\xi)}{s + (d_k + d_{k+1} - 2s)\,\xi(1-\xi)} .$$

**The in-bin derivative.** Put $u = \xi(1-\xi)$, $N = s\xi^2 + d_k u$, $D = s + \delta u$ with $\delta = d_k + d_{k+1} - 2s$, primes denoting $d/d\xi$. Then $dy/dx = (h/w)(N'D - ND')/D^2 = s\,(N'D - ND')/D^2$, and expanding with $u' = 1 - 2\xi$ and the identity $2\xi u - \xi^2(1-2\xi) = \xi^2$ gives $N'D - ND' = s\,[\,2s\xi + d_k(1-2\xi) + \delta\,\xi^2\,] = s\,[\,d_{k+1}\xi^2 + 2s\,\xi(1-\xi) + d_k(1-\xi)^2\,]$, so

$$\frac{dy}{dx} \;=\; \frac{s^2\,\big[\,d_{k+1}\xi^2 + 2s\,\xi(1-\xi) + d_k(1-\xi)^2\,\big]}{\big[\,s + (d_k + d_{k+1} - 2s)\,\xi(1-\xi)\,\big]^2},$$

which is the `dnum` over `den` squared that `_rq_spline` returns as a log. At $\xi = 0$ it is $s^2 d_k / s^2 = d_k$ and at $\xi = 1$ it is $d_{k+1}$, so the map is $C^1$ across knots. Since $y$ runs from $y_k$ to $y_k + h$ across the bin, the average of $dy/dx$ over the bin is $s$. The denominator can be written $s\,[\xi^2 + (1-\xi)^2] + (d_k + d_{k+1})\,\xi(1-\xi)$; for positive knot derivatives it and the numerator are positive, and the map is strictly increasing.

**Equal knot derivatives.** With $d_k = d_{k+1} = d$ and $t = 2\xi(1-\xi) \in [0, \tfrac12]$, the derivative is $s^2\,[\,d(1-t) + st\,]/[\,s(1-t) + dt\,]^2$, whose derivative in $t$ is $-s^2(d - s)\,(m + 2n)/m^3$ with $n = d(1-t) + st > 0$ and $m = s(1-t) + dt > 0$. It is therefore monotone between its knot value $d$ at $t = 0$ and its midpoint value

$$\frac{dy}{dx}\Big|_{\xi = 1/2} \;=\; \frac{2s^2}{d + s}$$

at $t = \tfrac12$, falling when $d > s$ and rising when $d < s$; it never exceeds $\max(d,\, 2s^2/(d+s))$. Knot derivatives $d \gg s$ at both ends of a bin thus give the map a derivative $d$ at the knots, $d/s$ times the bin's average, and a derivative of order $s^2/d$ at the bin's middle. On a uniform-base row of the block whose output is the base point, the density of that block's input on the row, given the rows before it, is half the derivative, so this is a density spike at the knot and a dip inside the bin.

**Free derivatives and the bin slope.** Under free derivatives $d_k$ and $s_k$ are separate outputs of the conditioner: a knot derivative is not a function of the slopes of the two bins it joins, and the ratio of a bin's knot derivatives to its mean slope is not bounded above. Free derivatives are floored at $d_{\min}$, and $s_k$ at the height floor divided by $1 - (K-1)\ell_0$, since the other $K-1$ width fractions are each at least $\ell_0$. With no per-row floor (heights floored at $\ell_0$, and $\ell_0 = d_{\min}$) that exceeds $d_{\min}$; a height floor below $d_{\min}(1 - (K-1)\ell_0)$ admits bins with $s_k < d_{\min}$, and in such a bin both free knot derivatives exceed $s_k$.

**The in-bin inverse.** Inside one bin `rq_spline_inverse` solves the in-bin map above for $\xi$ as the quadratic $a_2\xi^2 + a_1\xi + a_0 = 0$ with $a_2 = h(s - d_k) + Y\delta$, $a_1 = h d_k - Y\delta$, $a_0 = -sY$, $Y = y - y_k$, $\delta = d_k + d_{k+1} - 2s$, taking the root $\xi = 2a_0/(-a_1 - \sqrt{a_1^2 - 4a_2a_0})$.

## The height floor

`--min_bin_height` $h_0$ sets the height floor $\ell$ of `_bin_fractions` on the rows `--min_bin_height_rows` names: `uniform` for the wrapped and bounded rows (`_Block.unit_base_rows`), `all` (the default) for every row. Rows outside the choice keep $10^{-3}$, and with `--min_bin_height none` every row keeps $\ell_0$, the floor `_rq_spline` uses when it is given no per-row floor (resolved values when left unset: run settings section). Widths keep `_MIN_BIN` in every case. `InnModeller.init_gfn` raises `ValueError` unless $0 < h_0 < 1/K$, with the message that at or above $1/n_{\text{bins}}$ the heights cannot move or go negative; raises `ValueError` for a `--min_bin_height_rows` other than `uniform` or `all`; refuses the floor with `--w_path` above 0 (`NotImplementedError`, with the message "the reparameterised path gradient is unbounded inside floored bins"); and prints `bin-height floor ... on ... rows (else 1e-3)`. It sets `min_height` on every block, a per-row tensor.

On the trainer's NF route the floor is `cfg:model.nf.min_bin_height` on the rows `cfg:model.nf.min_bin_height_rows` names. `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy` raises `ValueError` (`flow min_bin_height ... must lie in (0, 1/n_bins)`) unless $0 < h_0 < 1/K$, applies the floor to the uniform-base rows when the rows setting is `uniform` and to every row for any other value, keeps $10^{-3}$ on the rest, registers the per-row floor on each block as a non-persistent buffer `min_height`, and prints nothing.

What a height means depends on the row's base. Take the block whose output is the base point: the last block of the stack, or the only one at $n_{\text{blocks}} = 1$. Given the rows before it in the order, the probability that a row's value falls in bin $k$ (in the coordinate the spline reads, the row plus its shift: rotated on a wrapped row, translated on a linear row, unshifted on a bounded row) is the base mass of $[y_k, y_{k+1}]$. On a uniform-base row that is $h_k/2$ on an interval of length 2, which is the bin's height fraction: the height fraction is the bin's share of the row's conditional mass. The floor is then a minimum mass per bin, the $K$ floors together fix $K h_0$ of the mass whatever the weights, and the softmax distributes the remaining $1 - K h_0$. On a Gaussian-base row the height is a length $2b \times$ fraction in base units, and its mass $\Phi(y_{k+1}) - \Phi(y_k)$ depends on where the bin sits: up to $h_k\,\phi(0)$ near the base's centre, and of order $h_k\,\phi(b)$ near the edge of the box. For an earlier block of a stack the output is the next block's input, whose distribution is neither uniform nor Gaussian, and the height fraction of a bin is not its mass.

## Knot derivatives: free and tied

**Free** (`energies/prior_flow.py::_knot_derivatives`, used whenever the derivatives are not tied). Each derivative slot gives $d = d_{\min} + \mathrm{softplus}(\text{slot})$, unbounded above. Per row kind: a wrapped row takes its $K$ slots as $d_0, \dots, d_{K-1}$ and sets $d_K = d_0$; a linear row pins $d_0 = d_K = 1$ and takes $d_1, \dots, d_{K-1}$ from slots $0, \dots, K-2$, leaving slot $K-1$ unread; a bounded row takes $d_0, \dots, d_{K-1}$ from its slots and $d_K = d_{\min} + \mathrm{softplus}(\text{shift slot} + \kappa)$.

**Tied** (`energies/prior_flow.py::_tied_derivatives`, `--tied_derivatives`). The derivatives are functions of the bin slopes. At knot $k$, joining bins $k-1$ and $k$ (bin $K-1$ at $k = 0$),

$$d_k \;=\; \frac{2\,s_{k-1}s_k}{s_{k-1} + s_k}\; f_k, \qquad f_k \;=\; \exp\!\big(\log F \cdot \tanh(\text{slot}_k - \kappa)\big) \in [1/F,\ F],$$

with $F$ = `--tied_factor` (2 when unset and not taken from a checkpoint; `ValueError` below 1; $F = 1$ gives $f_k = 1$, the pure harmonic mean; for $F > 1$ the factor lies strictly inside the interval in exact arithmetic, $\tanh$ never reaching $\pm 1$, while in floating point $\tanh$ rounds to $\pm 1$ at large arguments and the factor can reach $F$ or $1/F$). In `_rq_spline` the argument `tied` is the factor range, `True` meaning 2. Ends per row kind: a wrapped row uses the formula at $k = 0$, joining bin $K-1$ to bin 0 across the seam, and sets $d_K = d_0$; a linear row pins $d_0 = d_K = 1$ and leaves slot 0 unread; a bounded row sets $d_0 = s_0 f_0$ and $d_K = s_{K-1}\exp(\log F \cdot \tanh(\text{shift slot}))$. $d_{\min}$ does not enter the tied derivatives. `InnModeller.init_gfn` refuses `--tied_derivatives` with `--mix_k`, sets `tied` on every block to $F$, and prints `knot derivatives tied to the neighbouring bin slopes (harmonic mean x a factor in [1/F, F])`.

On the trainer's NF route the mode is `cfg:model.nf.tied_derivatives` with $F$ = `cfg:model.nf.tied_factor`. `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy` sets `tied` on every block to `float(tied_factor)`, raises `NotImplementedError` (`flow tied_derivatives with mix_k`) with `mix_k`, does not check the factor against 1 and prints nothing: a `tied_factor` of 0 makes `tied` false and `_rq_spline` then uses free derivatives; a value in $(0, 1)$ gives $\log F < 0$, so the factor still lies in $[F, 1/F]$ with the slot's sign reversed; a negative value fails in `math.log` at the first spline evaluation.

**The harmonic mean of two slopes.** For $a, b > 0$ with $m = \min(a,b)$ and $M = \max(a,b)$, $2ab/(a+b) = 2mM/(m+M)$, which is at least $2mM/2M = m$ (with equality when $m = M$) and below $2mM/M = 2m$. A tied interior derivative therefore lies in $[m/F,\ 2Fm)$ for $m$ the smaller of its two adjacent slopes: it is less than $2F$ times either adjacent slope and positive. The same holds at the seam of a wrapped row, and a bounded row's end derivatives lie within a factor $F$ of their own bin's slope.

**The in-bin bound.** Suppose both knot derivatives of a bin are positive and at most $2Fs$, $F \ge 1$. In the rewritten denominator, $\xi^2 + (1-\xi)^2 \ge \tfrac12$ and the second term is non-negative, so the denominator is at least $s/2$. The bracket in the numerator is a combination of $d_{k+1}$, $2s$ and $d_k$ with weights $\xi^2$, $2\xi(1-\xi)$, $(1-\xi)^2$ summing to 1, so it is at most $\max(d_k, d_{k+1}, 2s) \le 2Fs$, and the numerator is at most $2Fs^3$. Hence

$$\frac{dy}{dx} \;\le\; \frac{2Fs^3}{(s/2)^2} \;=\; 8F\,s$$

everywhere in the bin. Under tied derivatives the hypothesis holds in every bin of a wrapped or bounded row and in bins $1, \dots, K-2$ of a linear row; the two end bins of a linear row have one end pinned to 1, which is not related to their slope, and are excluded. In the equal-derivative case above the derivative is at most $\max(d, 2s^2/(d+s)) \le \max(2Fs, 2s) = 2Fs$, below the general bound.

The tests in `tests/models/test_rq_spline_tied.py` pin these on random parameters with $K = 32$, in double precision with a height floor of $10^{-5}$ on every row: `tests/models/test_rq_spline_tied.py::test_pure_factor_is_the_harmonic_mean` checks $F = 1$ against the harmonic mean, `::test_knot_derivative_bound_with_negative_control` checks interior knot derivatives against $2F$ times the slope of the bin to their right at $F = 1$ and $F = 2$, and asserts that under free derivatives on the same parameters the largest such ratio exceeds 100, `::test_density_inside_bins_bounded_with_negative_control` checks $dy/dx \le 8Fs$ at $F = 2$ on bins $1, \dots, K-2$ of every row kind, and asserts that under free derivatives the largest ratio of $dy/dx$ to $s$ exceeds $10^3$, `::test_tied_inverts_and_logdet_matches_autograd` checks, at `tied` equal to `True`, 1 and 3, the log-derivative against the autograd derivative to $10^{-8}$ and the inverse to $10^{-5}$, and `::test_tied_true_is_factor_two` checks that `tied=True` is $F = 2$.

**Identifications.** The in-bin map is the rational-quadratic interpolant of Gregory and Delbourgo (1982), monotone for any positive knot derivatives; the choice of those derivatives for it is the subject of Delbourgo and Gregory (1985). Neural Spline Flows (Durkan et al. 2019) use the same interpolant as a flow transform, with widths and heights from a softmax, interior derivatives from a softplus of an unconstrained network output, and end derivatives of 1 meeting identity tails: the free branch here, on linear rows. A circular spline with matched end derivatives composed with a rotation is the circular spline flow of Rezende et al. (2020). The tied rule's harmonic mean is the derivative of Fritsch and Butland (1984) for monotone piecewise cubic Hermite interpolation, whose general form, a formula due to Brodlie described there, weights the two reciprocal slopes by the interval lengths and reduces to the harmonic mean on equal intervals; `_tied_derivatives` uses the unweighted mean whatever the two bin widths.

## What is a run setting and what is a weight

`inn_modeller.py::resolve_auto_options`, called in `InnModeller.init_gfn` after the GFN probe and the read of any `--init_from` checkpoint's `inn` entry, fills the options left unset on the command line (`--bounded_rows`, `--min_bin_height` and `--block_orders` default to `auto`, `--tied_derivatives`, `--tied_factor` and `--bank_frac` to none) and prints `options left unset, resolved: ...` with the source of each value. On an unconditional run without `--init_from` the values are `inn_modeller.py::UNCONDITIONAL_DEFAULTS`: bounded rows `linear`, floor $10^{-7}$, tied derivatives with $F = 2$, and the order `id` (the stored order, cell rows first) when the flow has one block, else the alternating orders, whose first block is the stored order too. On a conditional run they are off: no bounded rows, no per-row floor, free derivatives, the alternating orders. With `--init_from` they are the checkpoint's saved `inn` entry, a key absent there giving no bounded rows, no per-row floor, free derivatives and the alternating orders; `--bank_frac`, a training setting, takes the route's default either way: on an unconditional run the 0.1 of `inn_modeller.py::UNCONDITIONAL_DEFAULTS`, on a conditional run 0. `--no-tied_derivatives` turns the tied derivatives off explicitly. On the trainer's NF route `models/nf_policy.py::resolve_flow_settings` fills `cfg:model.nf.bounded_rows`, `cfg:model.nf.min_bin_height`, `cfg:model.nf.tied_derivatives` and `cfg:model.nf.tied_factor` from `models/nf_policy.py::UNCONDITIONAL_DEFAULTS` (bounded rows `linear`, floor $10^{-7}$, tied derivatives, and $F = 2$ whenever `tied_factor` is null) on unconditional and conditional policies alike, and `cfg:model.nf.block_orders` `auto` as `id` on an unconditional one-block flow, else the alternating orders; `none` turns `bounded_rows`, `min_bin_height` or `block_orders` off, and it reads no checkpoint.

The option settings are not in the state dict. `min_height` and `tied` are plain attributes of each block, set by `InnModeller.init_gfn`; on the NF route `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy` sets them from the settings, `min_height` as a buffer registered non-persistent. The bounded-row and uniform-base masks (`is_bounded` on each block and on the flow, `unit_base` on the flow) are buffers registered non-persistent; the half-period rows are a plain list on `LatentFlow`. The state dict does carry `mu`, `sd`, `is_wrap`, the per-row `bound` buffer, whose entries are 1.0 on bounded rows, and the MADE mask buffers and the transformer conditioner's `order` buffer, which encode each block's order. The `--tf_norm` choice shows in the state dict's keys (LayerNorm has a weight and a bias, RMSNorm a weight, the identity neither), and under `--init_from` the strict `load_state_dict` in `InnModeller.train` raises on a checkpoint built with a different norm. `InnModeller.save` writes the run's flags beside the weights as the checkpoint's `inn` entry. The same weights under a different floor or derivative mode are a different density: under tied derivatives a derivative slot is a factor on a harmonic mean, under free derivatives the argument of a softplus.

With `--init_from`, `InnModeller.train` loads the state dict and compares the checkpoint's `inn` entry with the run's flags. A different height floor or floor rows prints `WARNING --init_from: the checkpoint was trained with bin-height floor ...`, and a different derivative mode or factor range prints `WARNING --init_from: the checkpoint's knot derivatives were ...` ending `the derivative slot changes meaning`; the run continues in both cases. A transformer conditioner whose loaded `order` differs from this run's order for that block raises `ValueError` naming `--block_orders`. On the NF route `train.py::Modeller._build_gfn_config` stores the policy entry resolved by `models/nf_policy.py::resolve_policy_entry`, and a resume compares it with the checkpoint's (`checkpointing.py::Checkpointer._gfn_config_from`), raising `ValueError` on any difference except the seed and the keys of `models/nf_policy.py::PRECISION_ONLY_KEYS` (`tf_fp32`), which follow the run's config.

## Conditional runs

On a conditional run (a config that gives each row a condition vector, or `--t_factor` above 1), the sampler is `models/spline_flow.py::ConditionalLatentFlow`, whose blocks (`models/spline_flow.py::_ContextBlock`) read an embedding of the condition through `models/spline_flow.py::_ContextMADE` or a transformer conditioner built with `context_dim`; its wrapped and bounded rows take the uniform base and every other live row the Gaussian. `InnModeller.init_gfn` refuses `--block_orders`, `--fold_half_rows`, `--bounded_rows` and `--mix_k` there with `NotImplementedError`, so a conditional flow has the alternating orders and no bounded rows. The class itself takes bounded rows (`ConditionalLatentFlow`'s `bounded_rows`, each a live linear row, uniform base and no tails as above); `inn_modeller.py` never passes them, and `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy` passes every live linear row by default and refuses `block_orders`, `fold_half_rows` and `mix_k` on a conditional policy with `NotImplementedError`. `--min_bin_height` and `--tied_derivatives` apply as on an unconditional run; on the NF route they default to the floor $10^{-7}$ and tied derivatives with $F = 2$ on a conditional policy too.

On an FD run over several space groups the state is the slot layout of `energies/fd_coordinates.py::FDSlotLayout`, and `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy` (built with `fd_space_groups` naming two or more groups; `NotImplementedError` off the conditional molecule route, `ValueError` unless it reads each row's group through a `cfg:model.nf.sym_ops` other than `none` or a space-group condition column) sets each row's live mask from its space group (`energies/fd_coordinates.py::FDSlotLayout.live_mask` passed to `models/spline_flow.py::_ContextFlow.set_row_live`, by row position, so a scrambled condition does not move a row's pads). `_ContextBlock.forward` takes a row's pad slots in and out as 0 and adds nothing to the log-determinant for them, the standardisation and base terms count live slots only (`ConditionalLatentFlow.to_base`, `ConditionalLatentFlow.log_prob`), and every inverse pins them at 0 as it solves, so $\log q$ is the density of a row's live slots (`tests/models/test_nf_mixed_groups.py::test_pads_are_drawn_as_zero_and_log_q_is_the_live_slots_density` checks it against the autograd Jacobian of each row's live slots under plain, typed and prefix rows, and `::test_a_pads_value_does_not_reach_log_q` that a pad's value leaves $\log q$ unchanged). With `cfg:model.nf.row_tokens` `typed` or `prefix`, typed rows then come from `models/fd_slot_describer.py::SlotRowDescriber`: each row's own group's `models/fd_row_describer.py::FDRowDescriber` features in its slots (0 on its pads), followed by a one-hot of the row's coordinate type and a pad flag; the per-slot types handed to the conditioner name each slot by the widest group of its block.

`models/spline_flow.py::_ContextFlow.context_input` returns what the blocks and the level head read. Without atom tokens that is the standardised condition vector passed through a residual MLP (`context`), whose output, projected to the transformer width, a transformer conditioner adds to every token (`CausalTransformerConditioner.forward`), and the standardised vector itself for the level head.

With `--atom_tokens`, which requires the transformer conditioner, `InnModeller.pack` puts the condition id in column 0 of the packed condition. `models/spline_flow.py::AtomEncoder` encodes that id's molecule from a table of atomic numbers and standard-frame coordinates filled from the conditions files (`InnModeller.note_atoms`). It runs an mxtaltools `ScalarGNN` over each atom's atomic number and coordinates on a radius graph within the molecule (`--atom_cutoff`), and gives one token per atom (the node embedding with an embedding of the atom's coordinates, projected to the transformer width) and an attention-pooled vector which, joined by any further columns of the packed condition (the temperature column on a `--t_factor` run, unstandardised), takes the standardised condition vector's place as the input of `context` and of the level head.

On the trainer's NF route the setting `atom_tokens` (`models/nf_policy.py::ATOM_TOKEN_MODES`) chooses the encoder:

- `True`: the `AtomEncoder` above.
- `'split'`: `models/spline_flow.py::SplitAtomEncoder`. Its invariant trunk (a `ScalarGNN` on atomic numbers, with geometry entering only through interatomic distances, and under `atom_chirality`, on by default, `models/nf_policy.py::CHIRAL_CHANNELS` pseudoscalars from `models/spline_flow.py::ChiralChannels`) gives a pool $s$; its point stream (atomic number, trunk features and standard-frame coordinates) gives the atom tokens and a pool $p$. The level head reads $s$, and the context $s$ and $p$.
- `'split_pooled'`: the split encoder with no cross-attention.
- `'scalars'`: described below.

The condition columns after the id (temperature, space group, Z') are standardised by `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy._context` before the flow reads them.

The table is built per batch by `::NFPolicy._context`: when the rows carry identifiers, `::NFPolicy._molecule_table` gives one table row per distinct molecule (its atoms from the molecule's first row, kept per molecule set) and column 0 becomes the row's molecule, so each molecule is encoded once and `models/spline_flow.py::_atom_graph` reuses the set's radius graph; the typed rows' radii and prefix inputs, which `models/spline_flow.py::_ContextFlow.row_extra` reads by condition id, are then per molecule too. It falls back to one table row per graph without identifiers, under `rotate_frames` or `mirror_frames`, under `mol_scalars`, or with a space-group embedding (`sym_ops` other than `'none'`), and, with a printed notice, for the rest of the run once rows of one identifier carry different atomic numbers or atom counts, or coordinates more than `::NFPolicy.MOLECULE_TABLE_POS_TOL` (1e-3 Å) apart (`tests/models/test_nf_molecule_table.py`).

The NF route's `atom_tokens` `'scalars'` keeps the molecule route (column 0 the condition id, and the per-molecule radii the typed rows read) but builds no encoder: the context and the level head read only the molecule scalars (`models/nf_policy.py::MOL_SCALARS`, when `mol_scalars` is set) and the condition columns, and the blocks have no atom tokens to cross-attend to.

Two widths are settings on the NF route (`models/nf_policy.py::NF_PENDING_DEFAULTS`, not in mk_dev): `tf_mlp_ratio` sets each transformer layer's MLP width to `tf_width` $\times$ `tf_mlp_ratio` (default 4), and `level_hidden` the level head's width (default 256); `inn_modeller.py` builds the flow with these default values.

The NF route's `cond_bottleneck` and `cond_noise` (`models/nf_policy.py::NF_PENDING_DEFAULTS`) squeeze each pooled molecule vector (the split encoder's $s$ and $p$, the xyz encoder's pool) through a linear map to `cond_bottleneck` dims and a tanh before the context and the level head read it, adding noise $\mathcal N(0, \sigma^2)$ with $\sigma$ = `cond_noise` per row in training mode only (`models/spline_flow.py::_ContextFlow._squeeze`). Noise without a bottleneck is refused, and both are refused without an atom encoder (`atom_tokens` false or `'scalars'`) (`tests/models/test_nf_cond_bottleneck.py`).

The NF route's `rotate_frames` (`models/nf_policy.py::NF_PENDING_DEFAULTS`; it needs the FD chart and an atom encoder, `atom_tokens` `True`, `'split'` or `'split_pooled'`, and is refused on the K-cut groups `energies/fd_coordinates.py::KCUT_GROUPS` by `energies/fd_coordinates.py::frame_rows`, which gives the orientation rows) is the same augmentation as `--rotate_frames` below, inside the policy step. In training mode `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy._frames` draws a frame per row, uniform on $\mathrm{SO}(3)$, on every forward, backward and replay call; the condition carries its quaternion so the encoder reads the rotated atoms, and the orientation rows are re-expressed for it by `energies/fd_coordinates.py::rotate_orientation` ($R R_0^\top$ on the hemisphere chart, on the state's device; no other chart row moves; `tests/crystal/test_fd_reframe.py` checks it against the exact `FDCoordinates.reframe` on space groups 1, 2, 4, 14, 15, 19, 33 and 61), backward on the stored row and inverted on a forward draw (`::NFPolicy._present`). States stay in the standard frame and $\log q$ is the standard-frame density: the flow's $\log q$ of the re-expressed row plus the map's log-Jacobian, the Haar density ratio (`::NFPolicy._one_step`; `tests/models/test_nf_rotate_frames.py`). Eval mode presents the standard frame. The eval draws (`train.py::Modeller.fwd_eval_sampling`) keep the policy in training mode and run inside `::NFPolicy.standard_frames`, which presents the standard frame and leaves every other training-mode behaviour as it is; before 2026-11-13 they were drawn in random frames (`tests/models/test_nf_frames_warm_start.py`). A training step with `scramble_conditions` raises `NotImplementedError`. The flow is not equivariant by construction; `train.py::Modeller.density_readouts` also scores the first `train.py::FRAME_SPREAD_ROWS` (1024) fixed rows of each readout set in `train.py::FRAME_SPREAD_K` (4) random frames, fixed per eval by the seed (`models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy.log_density` with `frames`), and logs `<set>_frame_spread`, the mean over rows of the standard deviation of $-\log q$ across the frames, `<set>_frame_shift`, the mean over rows of the frames' mean minus the standard frame's, both in nats and 0 for a frame-independent density, `<set>_frame_mean` and `<set>_frame_p90`, the mean and the 90th percentile over rows of each row's mean $-\log q$ across the frames (the random-frame level and tail; `train.py::frame_readouts`). No parameter depends on the frame flags (the context reads the pooled molecule and the condition columns after the frame), so a weights-only warm start may switch them on or off ([checkpoints-and-resume](checkpoints-and-resume.md)).

The NF route's `mirror_frames` (`models/nf_policy.py::NF_PENDING_DEFAULTS`) has the same requirements and is refused by `energies/fd_coordinates.py::mirror_rows` on a group without an entry in `energies/fd_coordinates.py::MIRROR_MAPS` (space groups 2, 4, 14, 15, 19, 33 and 61; the K-cut groups, Cc among them, are refused). In training mode `NFPolicy._frames` appends a fifth condition column, a parity of $-1$ with probability $1/2$ (the frame's rotation is the identity unless `rotate_frames` is also set); the conditional flow, built with `mirror`, reflects the molecule's atoms, $X \to \mathrm{diag}(-1, 1, 1)\,X$, before the frame's rotation, and `NFPolicy._present` replaces the row by its mirror image (`energies/fd_coordinates.py::mirror_state`, with its log-Jacobian) before the rotation, the inverse undoing the rotation first. `Modeller.density_readouts` then reflects every second readout frame and also logs `<set>_mirror_shift`, the mean over rows of $-\log q$ with the molecule reflected and no rotation minus the standard frame's (`tests/models/test_nf_mirror_frames.py`).

The NF route's `canonical_frame` (`models/nf_policy.py::NF_PENDING_DEFAULTS`, default `'none'`; `'inertial'` or `'learned'`, both needing `atom_tokens` `'split'` or `'split_pooled'` and the FD chart, refused on the K-cut groups) makes the presented frame cancel. The split encoder computes a proper frame $F$ from the atoms it is shown (`models/spline_flow.py::SplitAtomEncoder._frame`): `models/spline_flow.py::inertial_frame` takes the heavy-atom principal axes by ascending moment, signs each toward the far direction (a softmax over heavy-atom distances at `models/spline_flow.py::FAR_TAU`, so atoms tied for farthest share it and the frame stays equivariant through float32 rotation), and rebuilds the axis least aligned with it as the cross product of the other two, so $F$ is proper (a molecule with fewer than three heavy atoms uses all its atoms); on stored standard-frame molecules it is the identity to about $10^{-6}$ (2992 of 3000 pool8 training molecules; the rest are tie cases). `'learned'` adds to the first two inertial axes the sums $\sum_i a_k(h_i, |y_i - \bar y|)\,(y_i - \bar y)$ from a head on the invariant trunk features (its last layer zero, so it starts at the inertial frame), then Gram-Schmidt, and falls back to the inertial frame, with no gradient, when the shorter axis is below `models/spline_flow.py::FRAME_FLOOR` times the molecule's heavy-atom radius; a non-finite gradient into the learned frame (the quaternion step of `rotate_orientation` at the identity or a half turn) is zeroed (`models/spline_flow.py::_nan_safe`). The point stream reads the atoms in the frame, $F^\top(y_i - \bar y)$, and `models/spline_flow.py::_ContextFlow.context_frame` returns $F$ per row beside the context. `models/spline_flow.py::ConditionalLatentFlow.set_row_frame` (installed by the policy with its orientation rows) makes the blocks model the canonical row: `to_base` maps $R \to R F$ (`energies/fd_coordinates.py::rotate_orientation` with $F^\top$) and adds its log-Jacobian, and `sample` and `rsample` map a draw back. Under `rotate_frames` the presented frame $R_0$ then cancels, $R R_0^\top \cdot R_0 F(y) = R F(y)$, so $\log q$ does not depend on it; a parity $-1$ frame still presents the enantiomeric problem. `eval/nf_fit.py::rows_given_ctx` refuses such a flow (`tests/models/test_nf_canonical_frame.py`).

The NF route's MLE term can be split by row block (`models/nf_policy.py::NF_PENDING_DEFAULTS` `orient_loss_weight` 1.0, `orient_grad_balance` `'off'`, `orient_grad_rho` 1.0, parsed by `config_invariants.py::orient_split_problems`; conditional route, FD chart, `n_blocks` 1, refused elsewhere by `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy`). With one block in the chart's own order the orientation rows $x_O$ (`energies/fd_coordinates.py::frame_rows`) come last, so $\log q = \log q(x_C \mid c) + \log q(x_O \mid x_C, c)$, $x_C$ the cell and centroid rows; `models/spline_flow.py::ConditionalLatentFlow.log_prob` with `rows` returns the second term beside an unchanged $\log q$ (each row's base term, standardisation and log-derivative, plus a canonical frame's log-Jacobian), and `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy._one_step` adds a presented frame's log-Jacobian to it. `models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy.mle_orient_split`, read by `gflownet_losses.py::get_gfn_backward_loss` before its trajectory call, arms the split. A weight $w \ne 1$ makes the per-row term $-\log q - (w - 1)\log q(x_O \mid x_C, c)$. A balance (`'norm'` or `'pcgrad'`) hands `train.py::Modeller.step_loss` the two blocks' batch-mean losses, and `train.py::Modeller._balanced_backward` writes $\rho\, g_O/|g_O| + g_C/|g_C|$ as the parameters' gradient, the norms global over every parameter; `'pcgrad'` first removes from each unit gradient its component along the other when their cosine is negative (`gflownet_losses.py::combine_block_grads`). It logs `gradbal/norm_C`, `gradbal/norm_O`, `gradbal/cos` and `gradbal/ratio` ($|g_C|/|g_O|$). The balance needs the MLE term to be its branch's only term, unweighted and unclipped, on bwd or replay steps; `config_invariants.py::nf_orient_split_is_well_formed` refuses a split no active stage's MLE reads and a balance on a fused MLE stage, and `step_loss` raises on a fused step. `bwd/mle` stays $-\log q$; `bwd/mle_orient` and `bwd/mle_rest` log the two parts. The settings change no parameter, so a resume and a weights-only warm start take them from the run's config (`models/nf_policy.py::LOSS_ONLY_KEYS`; `tests/models/test_nf_orient_split.py`).

The level head (`ConditionalLatentFlow.level`, and `models/nf_policy.py::_LevelHead` on the NF route) reads a detached copy of its input, so its regression trains the head alone and not the atom encoder. Under `--atom_tokens`, and on the NF route under `atom_tokens` `True` or `'split'`, every causal layer of the conditioner (`energies/ar_transformer.py::_CausalLayer` built with `cross`) attends from each slot to all of the molecule's atom tokens after its causal self-attention (`energies/ar_transformer.py::_cross_attend`, padding masked); the output projection of that block starts at zero. A slot's parameters depend only on the rows earlier in the order and on the molecule, so the block's Jacobian stays triangular (`tests/models/test_inn_atom_tokens.py::test_cross_attention_keeps_the_order_causal`).

With `--rotate_frames`, which requires `--atom_tokens`, `InnModeller.pack` puts a unit quaternion in columns 1 to 4 of the packed condition: the frame the row's molecule is presented in, the identity unless `InnModeller.context_of` is given frames. `_ContextFlow.context_input` then encodes once per (molecule, frame) pair, with the molecule's standard-frame coordinates rotated by the quaternion's rotation $R_0$ (`models/spline_flow.py::quat_to_rotmat`). In the likelihood stage `InnModeller.step_loss` draws one frame per group of `--rows_per_molecule` rows (`inn_modeller.py::random_frames`, uniform on $\mathrm{SO}(3)$) and re-expresses the batch's stored rows for it with `inn_modeller.py::rotate_latent`, before the noise. The crystal builder applies the orientation $R$ of rows 9 to 11 to the molecule recentred on its heavy-atom centroid, so `rotate_latent` replaces $R$ by $R R_0^\top$ and leaves every other row as it is; it first clamps the orientation rows as `latent_to_cell_params` clamps them before building. Every other caller of `context_of` and `pack` (evaluation, sampling, the energy stage) passes no frames and reads the standard frame. The map from $x$ to its re-expressed rows is not volume-preserving in the chart's coordinates: its log-determinant is $\log J(x) - \log J(x')$, with $J \propto \sin^2(r/2)\sin\theta$ the Haar density in the orientation rows' polar angle $\theta$ and rotation angle $r$. The likelihood term of `step_loss` is the flow's $\log q$ of the re-expressed rows, without that log-determinant.

`ConditionalLatentFlow.sample` inverts a transformer block through `invert_block_cached` with the block's context: `CausalTransformerConditioner.decode` adds the context vector's projection to each slot's token, as `forward` does, and projects the atom tokens to keys and values once per layer for the whole decode. Inside `ConditionalLatentFlow.shared_context`, which `InnModeller.train` opens around each update's loss, `context_input` returns its first result for a repeat call on the same tensor with gradients enabled; calls without gradients (the draws') are not kept. The NF route does not open `shared_context`, so each `context_input` call there encodes the condition again.

## The flow as the trainer's one-step policy

`models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy` gives the flow the trajectory interface of `models/gfn.py::GFN` as a one-step policy: a trajectory is the pair of states $(s_0, x)$. `NFPolicy.get_traj_fwd` draws $x$ with `LatentFlow.sample` or `ConditionalLatentFlow.sample` without gradient, casts it to float32, folds the wrapped rows onto $[-1,1]$ and pins the dead rows. `NFPolicy._one_step` then scores the drawn $x$ with the flow's `log_prob`, the forward pass on the drawn rows, keeping the autograd graph in the flow's parameters, and returns $\log q(x)$ as the step's $\log P_F$, zeros as its $\log P_B$, and an $[n, 2]$ log-flow whose column 0 is the level and column 1 zero. The level is a `models/architectures.py::LearnableScalar` starting at 0 on an unconditional policy and `ConditionalLatentFlow.level` on a conditional one, a residual MLP of width `level_hidden` whose output layer starts at zero.

`NFPolicy.get_traj_bwd` scores a stored terminal state (detached, folded, dead rows pinned) with $s_0 = 0$, and `NFPolicy.get_traj_replay` a stored trajectory's last state with its first as $s_0$, both through `_one_step`. With `freeze_policy` the log-density is computed without gradient and only the level keeps one; with `scramble_condition_tiles` above 0 the context rows are permuted by tiles and the flow reads the context detached. `NFPolicy.log_density` returns the same $\log q$ without a graph. A gradient through the trajectory's states (`traj_grads`, `path_grad_last_k`), exploration noise, replay re-propagation and a drift force raise `NotImplementedError`.

Two terms train the NF policy on the energy's gradient, standard frame only (`models/nf_policy.py::NFPolicy._refuse_framed` raises under `rotate_frames`, `mirror_frames` or a `canonical_frame`). Score matching (`cfg:fwd_loss_coeffs.score`, `cfg:bwd_loss_coeffs.score`, `cfg:replay_loss_coeffs.score`, knee `score_beta`, absent = 0 and 10.0): `NFPolicy.score` returns $g_q = \partial \log q(x \mid c)/\partial x$ at the folded, pinned state with the graph kept (the attention on the math SDPA kernel, the only one with a double backward), and `gflownet_losses.py::score_matching_rows` takes per row $r = g_q - g_R$ on the live rows and the Huber of $|r|$ with knee $eta$ ($	frac12 |r|^2$ inside, $eta(|r| - eta/2)$ beyond), averaged over the rows whose $g_R = \partial \log R/\partial x$ is finite. On the forward rollout $g_R$ comes from the rollout's own reward call made with the gradient kept; on bwd and replay from `score_force`: `'live'`, a reward call with the gradient on the drawn rows (`gflownet_losses.py::live_reward_force`, bwd at the route's temperature, `train.py::Modeller._bwd_log_T`), or `'stored'` on replay, the force recorded at admission (`train.py::Modeller._admission_force_legs`, armed by `train.py::Modeller._replay_needs_force`); bwd `'stored'` is refused (`config_invariants.py::nf_aux_terms_are_well_formed`). The pathwise reverse KL (`cfg:fwd_loss_coeffs.pathwise`, `pathwise_grad_clip`): `NFPolicy.get_traj_fwd` with `pathwise` draws $x = T_	heta(z)$ from `ConditionalLatentFlow.base_sample` through `ConditionalLatentFlow.rsample` and keeps $\log q_{ar	heta}(x)$, the density with every parameter and the context detached (`NFPolicy._pathwise_draw`), so `gflownet_losses.py::pathwise_rows` takes the mean of $\log q_{ar	heta}(x) - \log R(x)$ over rows with a finite reward and its gradient is the sticking-the-landing estimator; `pathwise_grad_clip` caps each row's gradient norm in $x$. The trajectory returned holds the detached draw, scored as any other, and one reward call with the gradient kept serves the TB reward, the force and the pathwise term (`gflownet_losses.py::get_gfn_forward_loss`). Both terms go to `_aux_terms` on a call with gradients enabled and, for bwd and replay, a live stash; `train.py::Modeller.fused_train_step` adds them outside the frac-weighted mix at their own coefficients from the branches that trained that step, the forward ones whether or not the forward branch's TB terms are frozen to a Z sidecar, and `train.py::Modeller.step_loss` raises on terms another step type left. `tests/models/test_nf_score_pathwise.py` pins the score against finite differences, the row losses' hand values and the sticking-the-landing gradient.

The parameter groups are `forward_policy`, the flow's blocks; `conditions_embedding_model`, the context network and, on the molecule route, the atom encoder and any symmetry-operation encoder; `flow_model`, the level; and `t_model`, `s_model` and `backward_policy`, empty modules. `tests/models/test_nf_policy.py::test_a_forward_trajectory_is_one_step_with_exact_log_q` and `::test_log_density_is_the_backward_steps_log_pf` pin the step's terms.

## Could be tooling

- `--init_from` compares the height floor, the derivative mode and a transformer block's order, and takes all three density options from the checkpoint when they are left unset. It does not compare an explicit `--bounded_rows`, whose mask is not in the state dict while the `bound` buffer it sets is and loads from the checkpoint; `--fold_half_rows`; or a MADE block's order, which lives in its mask buffers and loads from the checkpoint without a comparison with `--block_orders`. The first block reads $(x - \mu)/\sigma$ on every unwrapped row, bounded rows included, and a bounded row reads the raw row only because `set_scaler` writes $\mu = 0$, $\sigma = 1$ there; a checkpoint trained without a row bounded loads its own `mu` and `sd` onto it, so its interval in $x$ is then $[\mu - \sigma, \mu + \sigma]$. A resume on the trainer's NF route compares every resolved setting (see run settings).
- No test checks that `LatentFlow.log_prob` normalises, that a bounded row has zero density outside $[-1,1]$, or that `per_dim_log_q` sums to `log_prob`, which is checked only inside `--dump_draws`. `tests/models/test_tf_norm.py::test_cached_decode_inverts_the_forward_pass` checks only that `sample` and `to_base` invert each other; `tests/models/test_inn_cached_sampler.py::test_cached_equals_dpass_unbounded_and_bounded` checks the cached inverse against the $d$-pass inverse in double precision; `tests/models/test_nf_mixed_groups.py::test_pads_are_drawn_as_zero_and_log_q_is_the_live_slots_density` checks a conditional flow's $\log q$ against the base log-density plus the log-determinant of the autograd Jacobian on mixed-group FD rows; and `tests/models/test_rq_spline_tied.py` covers the spline alone. No test exercises `invert_differentiable` or `rsample`, so the statement that autograd's derivative through the passes is the exact inverse's rests on the derivation above.
- The trainer's NF route has no start-up counterpart of `InnModeller.check_sampler_round_trip`; `tests/models/test_nf_policy.py::test_bounded_rows_linear_bounds_every_live_unwrapped_row` checks that a double-precision draw maps back to its base point within $10^{-9}$ on a flow with perturbed parameters. On that route a draw is decoded through the cache and scored by the full forward pass; with the layers in bfloat16 (`cfg:model.nf.tf_fp32` false) on CUDA the two round differently, and nothing on the route measures the resulting gap in $\log q$.
- The `_MADE` comment and the `_knot_derivatives` docstring describe the zero-initialised free spline as exactly the identity with slope 1; the code computes knot derivatives $1 + d_{\min}$ there. The comment beside the floor in `InnModeller.init_gfn` states a minimum mass per bin for every uniform-base row; the derivation above gives it for the block whose output is the base point.
- The in-bin test checks bins $1, \dots, K-2$ only, although the bound derived above also covers the end bins of wrapped and bounded rows.
