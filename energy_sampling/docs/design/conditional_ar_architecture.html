Figure 1

The condition embedding, computed once per rollout

One invariant vector s for everything that should not depend on how the molecule is oriented (log Z, the space-group choice), and one point vector p that carries the frame the orientation rows rotate from. Information runs from the trunk into the point head and never back.

Built in 9c2f59d8: models/spline_flow.py::SplitAtomEncoder, wired by NFPolicy when model.nf.atom_tokens is split or split_pooled. Tests: s and the level input are unchanged under rotation and reflection of the molecule, p and log q change (tests/models/test_nf_split_encoder.py). The old encoder (atom_tokens: true, coordinates in the node input) and the frozen Mo3ENet vector route still exist. Planned: the space-group head, and the SG leaving the context's columns once it is a token.

Figure 2

The AR flow over one crystal

A causal transformer reads the tokens in order. Each token's spline parameters depend only on earlier tokens and on the condition, so the density is exact and one parallel pass scores a whole crystal. The draw decodes one token at a time. Shown for P2₁/c, whose fundamental-domain chart has 10 coordinates.

Token names and kinds are the P2₁/c chart's, read from FDCoordinates(14, r).names and wrap_mask. Today the flow runs one block in the chart's own order (cell first, orientation last); the conditional route refuses other orders. The decode for sampling is KV-cached, one token per step. The SG token, the prefix feature and the p(g | s) term are planned.

Figure 3

Option C: one flow for every space group

Each space group has its own chart: 7 to 12 coordinates, in 11 different layouts across the 26 Day groups. Today a token's identity is its slot number. Slot 3 means s in P2₁/c and s_beta in P-1, so one network cannot serve both, and each run is fixed to one group. Option C replaces the slot number with what the coordinate is, and puts the space group first.

Chart names and kinds from FDCoordinates(14, r) and FDCoordinates(2, r) (s_gam is s_gamma). The transformer change is what the slide calls a positional embedding: a learned vector per coordinate name and kind in place of the per-slot vector. Three things outside the transformer must also become per-token: the spline's kind and bounds, the input projection (a circle feeds sin and cos), and the sum in log q, which skips padding. Sampling decodes to the batch's longest chart. Estimate: about a day with exactness tests per layout.
